# Data release notes

PBSInstr and PBSBench annotations were synthesized with GPT-4o and reviewed on sampled subsets. They may contain factual errors or hallucinations. Differential-estimation answers use classifier outputs as a surrogate reference and are not expert quantification.

These resources are for research and education only, not standalone diagnosis
or clinical decision support. Public annotations contain no credentials,
private absolute paths, direct patient identifiers, acquisition-system
identifiers, or unnecessary metadata. Source-native paths from already-public
external datasets are retained only where needed to resolve an annotation to
its image. Image redistribution depends on each source dataset’s terms.
